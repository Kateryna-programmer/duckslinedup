Who We Are

Small studio. Big attention to detail.

Ducks Lined Up is a small Kajabi design and development studio. We work with coaches, course creators, and online educators who want their site to actually make sense — not just look good.

That’s one of the reasons we love working in Kajabi. Your website, offers, emails, automations, forms, courses, and community all live under one roof. Together, they shape every step of your customer’s journey. We don’t see them as separate pieces — we see one business, and we think it should all work together.

By now, you’ve probably figured out we’re big fans of having our ducks in a row. For us, that means looking beyond individual pages and thinking about how the whole experience supports your business — before, during, and after someone buys. It’s a principle we bring to every project.
